# Home (stage screen)

Mode: Operate. One window, played live on a dark stage. Code-led build on a native platform; the locked decision comp `.impeccable/mocks/decision/r1-pick.webp` is the critique reference.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The home is a lit stage seen from the musician's side: black, with each drum pad casting a coloured beam through haze and the sounding tonal note burning as a flame. It refuses the dark-DAW panel of knobs and meters.

OWN-WORLD: Stage black ground (#050506), Liquid Glass panes that read as dark glass, near-white ink, flame gold (#FF8A2A to #FFD27A, white core) reserved for the sounding note and live tempo. Each pad's own colour is its beam. Hairline seams, no shadows. SF Pro, tabular digits.

STORY: The performer sees key and tempo at a glance, rides pad level, reverb and pulse from the stage strip, fires drums, and changes key; the flame always says what is sounding.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Glass toolbar (menu, set, pad and kit steppers, MIDI, settings). Glass stage strip: key monumental, BPM with beat lights and Tap, Pad and Reverb sliders, Pulse segmented, Stop isolated at the trailing edge. Left: 12 notes, 4x3, flame on the sounding one, others recede; Latch/Hold below. Right: 8 pads, 4x2, beams from the top edge.

FORM: Palco com fumaça, grounded candidate 1 (IMPECCABLE'S PICK), seed key e578f57f.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
